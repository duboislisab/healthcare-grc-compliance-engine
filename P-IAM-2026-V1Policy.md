# Staff Account Decommission Policy

## Purpose

To ensure that a departing team member's network, email, and building access are disabled promptly, while maintaining a documented HR record of each offboarding step.

## Scope

This policy applies to all full-time staff, interns, and contractors with organizational network, email, or building access.

Volunteers are not covered because they do not receive organizational network access. If a volunteer is granted organizational-system access in the future, this policy applies to that volunteer for the duration of that access.

## Process

All separations are tracked through a Staff Account Decommission ticket. The ticket automatically notifies HR at each documented milestone: when it is submitted, when the exit date and time are confirmed, and when accounts are disabled.

### Resignation or End of Contract

- As soon as the Manager learns the separation date, the Manager submits the ticket to IT/Security with that date.
- IT/Security confirms the exit date and time on the ticket. The default is 5:00 PM on the last day. If the team member's last working moment is earlier, the Manager requests an earlier time.
- At the confirmed time, IT/Security disables the network account, email account, and building access.

### Planned Termination

- The Manager submits the ticket to IT/Security, marked confidential. It is visible only to the Manager, HR, and assigned IT/Security staff.
- HR schedules the termination meeting and enters the date and start time on the ticket. IT/Security confirms that time as the disable time.
- At the meeting start time, IT/Security disables the network account, email account, and building access.
- HR completes the separation meeting and provides final-pay, benefits, and other required HR information.

### Immediate Termination

- The Manager submits the ticket to IT/Security marked `Immediate` and confidential.
- IT/Security disables the network account, email account, and building access immediately, and no later than 60 minutes after receiving the ticket.
- IT/Security records the actual disable time on the ticket.
- HR then provides final-pay, benefits, and other required HR information.

## Mailbox Access

When an account is disabled, IT/Security may grant the departing team member's Manager access to the mailbox for up to 90 days to handle business-critical communications. Access must be limited to business purposes and removed at the end of the approved period.

## HR Record and Ticket Closure

Once the ticket notifies HR that accounts are disabled, HR records the separation in the team member's personnel file.

IT/Security must document deprovisioning completion in the Staff Account Decommission ticket. The ticket remains in **Awaiting HR Verification** until HR confirms the correct person, exit time, and separation record. Only HR may close the ticket after this review.

## Exception Escalation

If any required system account cannot be disabled, IT/Security must keep the ticket open, record the error and affected system, and immediately alert the assigned IT/Security owner and HR. The exception is escalated according to the required completion deadline until all access is removed.
