# Risk Register Methodology
## Purpose and Scope

This document defines the methodology used to create, assess, maintain, and review the Healthcare Nonprofit Compliance Engine risk register. The register identifies cybersecurity, privacy, compliance, and operational risks that could affect the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and appropriate use of organizational systems and sensitive information.

This methodology establishes consistent definitions for register columns, risk ratings, control categories, ownership, key performance indicators, remediation priorities, review cadence, and risk acceptance decisions.
## Column Definitions

## Rating Methodology

### Likelihood Rating

Likelihood estimates how probable it is that the risk event will occur during the assessment period.

- **Low:** Unlikely to occur; strong controls are operating and there is little history of similar events.
- **Medium:** Could occur; control gaps, changing conditions, or prior events create a realistic possibility.
- **High:** Likely to occur; known weaknesses, frequent threat activity, or ineffective controls make the event probable.

### Impact Rating

Impact estimates the potential harm if the risk event occurs. Impact is scored on a scale from 1 to 10, considering effects on patient or sensitive information, operations, legal or regulatory obligations, finances, and organizational reputation.

### Inherent Risk Rating

Inherent risk is the level of risk before considering the effect of existing controls. It is determined by evaluating the risk’s likelihood and impact together.

- **Low:** Limited likelihood and limited impact.
- **Medium:** Meaningful likelihood or impact that requires monitoring and assigned ownership.
- **High:** Significant likelihood or impact that requires timely remediation and management attention.
- **Critical:** Severe potential harm, major regulatory exposure, or serious disruption requiring immediate escalation and corrective action.

## Review and Escalation
The risk register will be reviewed outside the scheduled cadence after a security incident, a material vulnerability finding, a significant system or vendor change, a control failure, or a material change in the threat environment.
The risk register is reviewed quarterly by designated risk owners and control operators. Reviews confirm the accuracy of risk ratings, control effectiveness, KPI results, remediation progress, and status.
A cross-functional Risk and Compliance Review Group coordinates quarterly risk-register reviews. Membership includes representatives from Information Security, IT, Compliance/Privacy, Human Resources, Operations, and department leadership as needed. The group reviews risk ratings, control effectiveness, KPI results, remediation progress, and open exceptions.
The Executive Director is the Risk Acceptance Authority for residual High and Critical risks. The cross-functional Risk and Compliance Review Group may approve and monitor residual Low and Medium risks. All accepted risks must be documented, time-bound, include compensating controls where applicable, and be reassessed at least quarterly.
Residual High and Critical risk acceptances expire after 30 calendar days and require Executive Director reapproval to continue.
Before renewing a residual High or Critical risk acceptance, the Executive Director must review relevant incidents and control failures, remediation progress and outstanding milestones, compensating-control effectiveness, and the cumulative number of prior approvals. The Executive Director may renew the acceptance, require additional safeguards, escalate remediation, or reject renewal.
